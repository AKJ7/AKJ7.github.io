Jo, hello :wave:!  

My name is Jules and i like everything about anything that does something.  

Formally speaking, i studied [mathematics](/posts/math-university-courses/) and mechanical engineering somewhere in Germany.
I recall one of my earliest memories to break apart my toys and try to reassemble them with extra unique features. This has basically shaped my interests and path through life.  

Besides mathematics and mechanical engineering, I find problems arising from electrical engineering, computer science and physics very interesting.
At 8 years old, i taught myself to solder and could finally fix diverse broken electronics in our little house.
To the world of software development and computers, i was introduced at 11.
A great hobby of mine thereafter was the developement of mobile app websites and webtemplates during the peak of the [Symbian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symbian) era and the rise of smartphones.  

During my studies, i worked as a software developer at a small firm in my city.
At which, i handled every problem that landed on my desk, including data science, frontend/backend development, data engineering and embedded system development. 
I also worked periodically at my university in research and latter on as an machine learning engineer. 

As an avid learner, i landed work in the embedded-systems branch for devices in automotive.
This has mark the start of my career.
It has given me the opportunity to bridge software and hardware development, to create fully mechatronic devices.
